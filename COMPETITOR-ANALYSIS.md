# Competitor Analysis — drosamajuma.com

Compiled 2026-09-29 from this session's cumulative `/seo-sxo`, `/seo-maps`, and keyword-research findings. Not a fresh crawl — treat as a synthesis of prior verified evidence, not new data.

## Top Competitors

### 1. EICADD
- **Type:** Autism/therapy center, institutional.
- **Positioning:** Explicitly states it serves **"الأردن والدول الخليجية"** (Jordan and Gulf countries) — the same combined market Dr. Osama targets.
- **Gap:** Institutional brand, no named individual expert; likely weaker on personal trust/E-E-A-T signals than a credentialed individual consultant.

### 2. Jordanian Autism Academy (الأكاديمية الأردنية للتوحد)
- **Type:** Autism-focused treatment center.
- **Positioning:** Treatment outcomes, not general educational consulting.
- **Gap:** Narrow niche (autism only) vs. Dr. Osama's broader inclusive-education/IEP/teacher-training scope.

### 3. Queen Rania Teacher Academy
- **Type:** Government-backed teacher training institution.
- **Positioning:** Dominates "تدريب معلمين التعليم الدامج" (inclusive-education teacher training) search results via institutional authority.
- **Gap:** No 1:1 consulting offer for families or institutions; hard to outrank on generic training queries head-on, so Dr. Osama's content should target the consulting/individual-guidance angle these institutions don't serve.

### 4. Mutah University / Yarmouk University academic programs
- **Type:** Formal diploma/certification programs.
- **Positioning:** Academic credentialing.
- **Gap:** Competes on formal certification, not ongoing consulting relationships or family-facing guidance.

## Cross-Cutting Findings

- **None of the 4 competitors have a personal, named, credentialed expert brand with an independent content library** — Dr. Osama's 36 published articles already exceed all of them combined on this dimension. This is a genuine, defensible content moat, not a gap to close.
- **None have a Google Business Profile for the specific local query pattern** ("استشاري تربية خاصة عمان" and similar) — confirmed via the `/seo-maps` audit this session. The local map-pack is currently open/uncontested.
- **Keyword-wording risk is competitor-specific, not universal:** this session's `/seo-sxo` work found a real wording mismatch ("الخطة التربوية الفردية" vs. "الخطة التعليمية الفردية") on `/iep/` via live SERP sampling — but explicitly tested and **disproved** that the same pattern applies to `/teacher-training/` or `/autism/`. Do not assume competitor-wording mismatches generalize across pages without re-verifying per page.

## Strategic Implication

The competitive fight here is not content volume (already won) — it's **trust infrastructure**: GBP, reviews, case studies, and testimonials, all of which are currently at zero for Dr. Osama specifically. Since the named institutional competitors ALSO don't compete well on the personal-brand/local-pack dimension, closing that specific gap (see `SEO-STRATEGY.md` Phase 1-2) is the highest-value use of near-term effort.
