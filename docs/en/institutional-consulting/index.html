Institutional Consulting

Developing Actionable, Measurable Inclusive Initiatives and Policies

Consulting helps an institution understand the gap between its stated commitment and the actual experience of people with disabilities, then turns priorities into policies, training, practices, and follow-up mechanisms. Work begins with a field assessment of the building, policies, and staff, followed by identifying priorities that are achievable within a single term, then periodic follow-up against pre-defined indicators. This consulting is also offered remotely to schools and institutions across the GCC.

Readiness Assessment and Setting Priorities

The assessment identifies strengths and barriers in policies, practices, access to information, the participation of people with disabilities, and staff capacity. Results are ranked by impact and feasibility.

  • Reviewing documents and practices
  • Interviews or working sessions
  • A gap and priority map
  • Actionable recommendations

Training and Inclusive Communication

Programs can be designed for leadership and staff, or a review of messaging and event language from a rights-based perspective. The goal is to avoid stereotypes and ensure information reaches, and people with disabilities participate with, dignity.

  • Respectful, rights-based language
  • Communication etiquette
  • More accessible events and content
  • Clear internal responsibilities

Governing the Initiative and Measuring Progress

Every action is linked to an owner, a deadline, and a follow-up indicator. The plan can be split into quick wins and medium-term changes, with people with disabilities involved in assessment and decision-making.

Stages of an Institutional Development Project

Turning policy into daily practice needs a clear workflow, not a single isolated decision.

  • An initial assessment of building, policy, and staff readiness
  • Identifying priorities achievable within a single term
  • Training relevant staff in parallel with adjusting procedures
  • Quarterly follow-up against pre-defined indicators
  • A comprehensive annual review of policy and results

Important Note

An educational decision based on need

No single plan fits everyone. The work starts with understanding the student and the learning environment, then defining measurable, trackable accommodations and support.

Frequently Asked Questions

Short, clear answers

Is the consulting only for schools?

No. It can be adapted for universities, organizations, public bodies, and HR, communications, and events teams depending on the project scope.

Does it include an engineering accessibility audit?

The overall experience can be reviewed and the need identified, but a detailed engineering or legal audit requires accredited specialists as part of the project scope.

Can the project start with a single workshop?

Yes, if the goal is focused awareness-raising. Broader institutional change usually requires a needs assessment and a follow-up plan after training.
