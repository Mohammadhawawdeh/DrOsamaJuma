Professional Profile

Educational expertise with a rights-based lens

Dr. Osama Juma works at the intersection of special education, inclusive education, and disability empowerment. His approach focuses on translating principles into classroom arrangements, individualized plans, and clear collaboration between family and teaching staff.

He holds a PhD in Special Education from the University of Jordan, with public contributions including university lectures, events, and media coverage related to inclusive education and accessibility.

What sets this approach apart?

  • A clear separation between educational support and medical diagnosis
  • Involving the student, family, and school in decisions
  • Measurable goals with regular review
  • Rights-respecting language that honors the individual and their dignity

Frequently Asked Questions

Before you get started

Are the services medical or diagnostic?

No. The site and services focus on inclusive education, educational guidance, individualized learning plans, teacher training, and institutional development. Medical or psychological diagnosis is the responsibility of licensed professionals and bodies.

Who can benefit from a consultation?

Families, teachers, schools, centers, organizations, and bodies working on inclusive education or the empowerment and accessibility of people with disabilities.

Can an IEP be prepared without school cooperation?

An initial educational outline can be provided, but a stronger plan needs the participation of the family, school, and relevant specialists, along with data on the student's level, goals, and learning environment.

Is training available for institutions outside Jordan?

In-person or remote programs can be discussed for organizations across the Arab region, depending on the topic, number of participants, and desired training outcomes.
