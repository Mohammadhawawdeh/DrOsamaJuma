Emergencies & Continuity

Inclusive Education During Emergencies and Distance Learning

A school closure or a sudden shift to distance learning tends to widen the gap for students with disabilities first, because it strips away support that was built into the physical classroom. Continuity needs a plan prepared in advance, not improvisation on the first day.

By Dr. Osama Juma — PhD in Special Education, The University of Jordan · Published: August 31, 2026 · 5 min read

Key References: UNESCO: Inclusion and Education During COVID-19 · UNICEF: COVID-19 and Children With Disabilities

What Should Be Ready Before an Emergency Happens?

Keep an updated contact list, a copy of each student's support plan, and a low-tech alternative (phone, printed packet, SMS) for a student without reliable internet or a suitable device.

Identify which parts of each student's support genuinely depend on physical presence - like certain therapies or physical assistance - so the plan can address them explicitly rather than dropping them silently.

How Do You Keep Learning Accessible at a Distance?

Offer material in more than one format (video, audio, text) and keep sessions or tasks short with clear, single-step instructions. A recorded lesson helps a student who needs repetition more than a live one does.

Maintain a regular, predictable communication channel with the family, since they become the main support bridge during distance learning, and check in on their capacity to fill that role.

How Do You Support the Family Without Overburdening Them?

Break tasks into short, guided steps a parent can supervise without needing teaching expertise, and prioritize a small number of goals instead of the full curriculum.

Watch for signs of fatigue or stress in the family and adjust the pace, and keep a return-to-school transition plan ready so the shift back doesn't undo what was gained.

Note: This content is general awareness/educational material. Determining the most appropriate support requires understanding the student's needs and learning environment, and collaboration with the relevant team.
