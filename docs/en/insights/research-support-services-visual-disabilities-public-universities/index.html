Published Research

Support Services for Students With Visual Disabilities in Public Universities

A published study by Dr. Osama Muhammad Juma and Professor Nadia Hael Al-Sarour examining the level of support services provided to students with visual disabilities enrolled in public universities.

By Dr. Osama Juma — PhD in Special Education, The University of Jordan · Published: 2023 · Volume 8, Issue 4 · 5 min read

Original source: This article was first published in the Jordanian Educational Journal – the Jordanian Educational Sciences Association. Read the full original version.

Publication Details

The research was published in the Jordanian Educational Journal, Volume 8, Issue 4, in 2023.

The journal's page records the authors and research details, and provides the abstract and the published version for reading or download.

What Is This Study About?

The study aims to identify the level of support services provided to students with visual disabilities enrolled in public universities.

Findings are examined by degree of disability and academic level, linking the assessment of the service to the diversity of students' needs and academic paths.

Why Does This Research Matter?

Visual disability requires an interconnected system that includes accessible materials, assistive technology, mobility, examinations, digital access, and faculty support.

For academic citation or to review the instrument and findings, use the original research page on the journal's site.

Note: This content is general awareness/educational material. Determining the most appropriate support requires understanding the student's needs and learning environment, and collaboration with the relevant team.
