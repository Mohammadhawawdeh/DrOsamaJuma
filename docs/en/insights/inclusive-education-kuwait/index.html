Inclusive Education in the GCC

A Student With a Disability's Rights in Kuwait: Practical Steps for Families

Successful inclusion in Kuwait, as anywhere else, starts with the family knowing its rights and asking the right questions before enrollment, not after a difficulty appears in the classroom.

By Dr. Osama Juma — PhD in Special Education, The University of Jordan · Published: September 6, 2026 · 4 min read

What Do You Need to Know Before Enrolling?

Public and private schools vary in how ready they are to welcome students with disabilities. Ask directly whether a prior educational assessment exists, and whether the school requires a specific report before admission.

Define the child's need precisely before the visit: is the need academic, motor, communication-related, or behavioral - this determines the most important questions to ask the school's administration.

What Does the Classroom Support Plan Look Like?

After admission, ask for a written plan that specifies classroom accommodations and who is responsible for following up on them, not just a verbal promise to “look into it.”

Follow up directly and regularly with the teacher; ongoing communication between the family and teacher is often more important than any formal item written into the plan.

When Do You Need Additional Support Outside the School?

If you feel the school alone isn't enough, combining school support with an independent educational consultation helps review the plan with an outside perspective and set clearer priorities.

Always ask for measurable indicators at every review: what concrete change has happened since the last meeting, not just a general description of improvement.

What Documents and Steps Does a Family Need?

Start with a current assessment report from a recognized specialist, since most schools and support programs will ask for updated documentation before building a plan - a report older than a year is often treated as out of date.

Request a written support plan from the school before the term starts, not a verbal assurance, and confirm who the ongoing point of contact will be if a concern comes up mid-term.

Note: Regulations and procedures for education and inclusion are subject to updates; check with official bodies and the school directly to confirm the latest developments.
